Questions?

Frequently Asked.

Do you charge extra for home visits?
+
No. All packages include home service at no extra charge. We come fully equipped to your location anywhere in Lucknow.
How far in advance should I book?
+
For bridal packages, we recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks in advance. For regular packages, same-day or next-day bookings are usually available.
What products do you use?
+
We only use branded, top-grade products — MAC, Lakmé, O3+, Schwarzkopf, L'Oréal, VLCC, and Lotus Herbals. We never compromise on what touches your skin.
Can I customise a package?
+
Absolutely. Call us and we'll build a package tailored to your occasion, skin type, and budget. We're flexible.
Are your artists certified?
+
Yes. Every artist on our team is trained and certified, with years of experience in bridal, party, and occasion makeup and skin services.
