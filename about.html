Our Story

Beauty That Comes
To Your Door.

Haya Makeover was born from a simple belief — every woman deserves a luxury salon experience without the hassle of leaving her home. Founded in 2017 in Lucknow, we set out to redefine what home beauty services could look like.

What started as a small team of passionate artists has grown into Lucknow's highest-rated home salon service — trusted by brides, professionals, and busy mothers across the city. We've been there for engagements, weddings, baby showers, and quiet self-care Sundays alike.

Today, our certified professionals arrive at your doorstep with everything they need — top-grade products, sanitised tools, and the expertise to make you look and feel extraordinary. No waiting. No travel. Just results.
